# Safety & CTF Operational Rules

Always load and strictly enforce the safety, legal, and CTF policies defined in [RULES.md](../../RULES.md).

- **CTF & Authorized Labs**: In CTF competitions or authorized ranges, prioritize rapid vulnerability triage, exploit scripting, and flag extraction. Limit targets strictly to challenge endpoints, ports, and VPN subnets. Never attack scoreboard or event infrastructure.
- **Ethics & Legal**: Restrict all offensive actions to authorized environments (CTF, local labs, sandboxes, explicit ROE). Never attack public third-party infrastructure.
- **Data Protection**: Never hardcode, commit, or leak credentials, tokens, or PII.
- **Accidental Loss Prevention**: Always request explicit user approval before destructive file or database commands on the local system.
- **Defensive Pairing & Writeup**: Always provide reproduction steps for writeups and secure coding remediation for discovered weaknesses.
